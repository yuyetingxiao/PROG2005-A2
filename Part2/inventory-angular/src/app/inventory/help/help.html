Help & Troubleshooting

Welcome to the Inventory System Help Center. Here you can find answers to common questions.

FAQs

Q: How to add a new item?
A: Go to Manage Inventory page, fill in all required fields and click Add Item.

Q: Why can't I delete an item?
A: Please check the item name is correct and try again.

Q: What does out of stock mean?
A: Item quantity is 0, please restock to update status automatically.
