Help & FAQ - Inventory Management System

Welcome to the help page for the Angular Inventory Management System. Here you will find answers to common questions, troubleshooting tips, and usage instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How do I add a new inventory item?

A: Go to the Inventory Operation page, fill in all required fields (ID, Name, Category, Supplier, Quantity, Price, Stock Status, Popular Item), then click the Add Item button. The item will be saved to your inventory list.

Q: How do I edit an existing item?

A: In the inventory list, find the item you want to edit and click the Edit button. The item's details will load into the form. Make your changes, then click Save Edit to update the item.

Q: How do I delete an item?

A: In the inventory list, find the item you want to delete and click the Delete button. You will be asked to confirm the deletion. Once confirmed, the item will be permanently removed from your inventory.

Q: How do I search for an item?

A: Go to the Search & Filter page, enter the item name in the search box, and the results will update automatically. You can also filter by category or show only popular items.

Q: Is my data saved if I close the browser?

A: Yes! All inventory data is stored in your browser's local storage, so it will persist even if you close the browser or restart your device. The data is only stored on your local device, not on any external server.

Q: Can I use this app on my mobile phone?

A: Yes! The app is fully responsive and designed to work on all mobile and desktop devices. The layout will automatically adjust to your screen size.

Troubleshooting

Problem: I can't add an item, it says "ID already exists"

Solution: Each item must have a unique ID. Please use a different ID that is not already in your inventory list.

Problem: My changes are not saving

Solution: Make sure you click the Save Edit button after making changes. Also, ensure your browser allows local storage (not in incognito mode).

Problem: The app is not working on my mobile device

Solution: Ensure you are using a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) on your mobile device. Clear your browser cache and reload the page.

Problem: I lost my inventory data

Solution: Data is stored in local storage, so clearing your browser cache or resetting your browser will delete the data. We recommend regularly backing up your inventory data if needed.
